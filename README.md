# blackjack-card-game
A simple command line-based card game where the objective to get the closest to 21. The player and dealer will both be dealt two random cards out of a 52-card deck, with the dealers second card being hidden. The player will then enter their turn and given the choice to either draw another card or stay.
Assuming the player stays below or on 21, the dealer will then continuously draw cards until their value is above 17, at 21, or goes over 21. Assuming both the player and dealer have a hand that is not over 21, it will then check to see who's value is closer to 21, ending the round and declaring a winner.
# Installation Instructions
1. Click the "Code" drop down menu top right  
2. Copy the HTTPS link  
3. Enter the following command to clone to your local machine:  
```git clone https://github.com/NAU-OSS/blackjack-card-game.git```
# Usage
When a compiled, view your current card total, what the dealer has, and decide to either hit or stay (by typing "H" or "S"). Once stay is selected, the dealer will then draw cards until total is above 17 or bust.
